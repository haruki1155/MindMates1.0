> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** This plan removes the duplicate mood button and the assessment card's X. It adds a "View today's mood" button under the greeting after a check-in and recolors the streak card's "Log mood" button to match its gold card.

**Architecture:** The changes are small edits to the Home widgets and screen. The button's visibility and target are decided in `HomeScreen` and passed in as a callback. The widgets only render.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter (Dart), Provider, `flutter_test`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-home-polish-40-41-design.md`
